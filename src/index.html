Your rides

Rides recorded on this device. Sign in under the rider menu to keep every ride on your account and send it to Strava or intervals.icu.

Load workouts

You can load several files at once:

  • .zwo Zwift workouts (also saved as .xml)
  • .erg and .mrc TrainerRoad, GoldenCheetah, PerfPRO and similar
  • .txt intervals.icu workout text, e.g. - 10m ramp 50-75%, 5x
  • .fit courses

.fit workouts and .gpx files are not supported yet.

About

Soigneur Studio: indoor cycling and training, on your own server.

Based on Auuki by Dimitar Marinov, which provides the trainer and sensor engine (Bluetooth, ANT+, FIT). Soigneur Studio is a separate project, not made or supported by Auuki.

Free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0. If you let others use your server over a network, offer them its source code.
