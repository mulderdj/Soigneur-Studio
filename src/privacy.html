Privacy

Soigneur Studio is software you run yourself. There is no company behind it that receives your data: whoever runs the server you use is in charge of it.

On your device

Your riders, workouts, rides (the last 7 as .fit files), ride summaries, training plan, health data and settings are kept in this browser's storage on this device. You can export rides as .fit files and remove a rider, with their data, from the rider menu.

On your account server

When you sign in, your account, profile (FTP, weight, W′, threshold heart rate), workouts, rides, plan, ratings and hidden rides are synced to the account server, so your other devices have them too. The server is the one you or your household run (for example with Docker Compose). Its administrator can see the accounts on it and can delete them.

Services you connect

Only if you connect them, per rider:

  • Strava and intervals.icu: new rides are uploaded to your account there. intervals.icu also sends your activities and planned workouts back, and receives the workouts you plan.
  • Garmin: with your own Personal API key, the server reads your health data (HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, Body Battery, stress, weight, VO2max) and activities. It only reads.

The keys and tokens for these services stay on the account server; they are not sent to your browser. Disconnecting removes them; the rides and data already brought in stay.

Bluetooth and USB

The browser asks before the app may use Bluetooth (trainer, sensors) or a USB ANT+ stick. Sensor data stays in the app and goes nowhere else unless you upload a ride.

Other requests

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when the page opens. There is no tracking, advertising or analytics.
